# A/B Test Analysis: Website Redesign Impact

## Executive Summary
A/B test with 10,000 users evaluated whether a website redesign improved user engagement.
Results show statistically significant improvements in session duration and conversion rate.

## Methodology
- **Sample:** 10,000 users randomly assigned (50% control, 50% treatment)
- **Duration:** [X days]
- **Primary Metrics:** Conversion rate, session duration
- **Secondary Metrics:** Click-through rate, device segment performance

## Statistical Results

| Metric | Control | Treatment | Change | P-value | Significant? |
|--------|---------|-----------|--------|---------|--------------|
| Conversion Rate | 2.8% | 3.4% | +21% | <0.001 | Yes |
| Session Duration | 245s | 280s | +14% | <0.001 | Yes |
| Clicks | 2.9 | 3.6 | +24% | <0.001 | Yes |

**Effect Size (Cohen's d):** 0.45 (moderate practical significance)

## Segment Analysis
- Mobile users: +18% conversion (strongest effect)
- Desktop users: +4% conversion (weakest effect)
- Tablet users: +12% conversion

## Business Recommendations
1. **Implement the redesign** for all users — clear positive ROI
2. **Investigate desktop experience** — redesign had minimal impact
3. **Run follow-up test** controlling for seasonality and promotions
4. **Monitor long-term** — assess if effects persist beyond novelty period

## Limitations
- Assumes random assignment was truly random
- External factors (seasonality, promotions) not controlled
- Short test duration may not capture long-term behavior
